# Internal QMS Audit Procedure

## 1. Purpose
Describe a consistent, impartial and risk-based approach to internal audits.

## 2. Audit programme
Establish an audit programme that considers:
- Process importance and changes affecting the organisation.
- Risks and opportunities.
- Previous audit results and recurring findings.
- Customer, regulatory and other applicable requirements.
- Planned audit scope, criteria, methods, responsibilities and timing.

## 3. Auditor competence and impartiality
Select auditors with appropriate competence. Auditors should be objective and impartial; where practicable, they should not audit their own work. Maintain evidence of relevant training, experience or competence evaluation.

## 4. Audit preparation
- Confirm objectives, scope, criteria and locations or functions.
- Review relevant procedures, previous findings and performance data.
- Prepare an audit plan and appropriate checklist.
- Confirm participants and interview arrangements.
- Identify records and evidence likely to be sampled.

## 5. Conducting the audit
Use interviews, observation and sampling of documented information. Record objective evidence and link each finding to the relevant audit criterion. Do not rely on unsupported assumptions.

## 6. Reporting
The audit report should identify:
- Audit date, scope, criteria and participants.
- Processes and areas sampled.
- Conformities and opportunities for improvement, where used.
- Nonconformities with objective evidence and requirement reference.
- Overall conclusions and follow-up expectations.

## 7. Follow-up
The responsible process owner should correct findings, analyse causes and plan corrective action as appropriate. Track agreed due dates and verify implementation and effectiveness before closure.

## 8. Records
Retain the audit programme, plans, checklists where used, evidence notes, reports, corrective action records and follow-up verification.
